1 Ma Sói Realtime — mobile app

Built from the UI/UX spec: dark-fantasy palette (#0F0E17 base, moonlight #A7C5EB accent, faction reds and greens), 16–20px radii, glow instead of drop shadow, Be Vietnam Pro for Vietnamese diacritics. Mobile-first at 402×874 in an iPhone frame, since players sit in the same room on phones.

All 17 roles are covered — the spec’s 10 plus seven added on request (Sói Tiên Tri, Sói Nguyền, Thám Tử, Con Quạ, Trưởng Làng, Thằng Ngố, Bà Đồng) — each with its own reveal card and, where the role acts at night, its own action screen. The normal player only sees what they are entitled to: the night order is anonymous, the death announcement gives no cause and no role, and the who-did-what-to-whom master log stays inside the Host Panel. Anti-peek is live on the two genuinely private cards, the Role Reveal and the night scry result. The loop runs Phát thẻ → Đêm → Rạng sáng → Tử vong → Thảo luận → Biểu quyết → Kết quả → Đêm kế tiếp, with the phase counter advancing; the match only reaches Game Over once a win condition lands or the host ends it. Because everyone is sitting at the same table, the card never sits open on screen: it is dealt face down, flipped only under a press-and-hold, and re-checked mid-game from a 🎴 button that shows it only while your thumb is down — wolves recognise their pack inside that same held view. The dead get a channel of their own; nothing they type reaches the living. Two night modes sit under the host’s control and they change the rules, not just the pacing: in order, the Witch is told who was bitten; simultaneous, she heals blind. Effects that land on you are reported privately at dawn, before the public announcement, and never name their source; effects that change the arithmetic of the vote — the Raven's mark, the Chief's double ballot, Cupid's pair — are marked in the open where everyone can weigh them. The one bridge across is the Bà Đồng, and it is deliberately narrow: she hears the dead only at night, never learns who spoke, and cannot answer.
